## The problem

Admins needed to see every apartment, floor, room and bed, move students between them, and manage waitlists. Students needed to upload identity and guardian documents from their phones. Different staff roles needed different powers.

## What I did

- Modelled a 4-level hierarchy (apartment → floor → room → bed) with waitlist allocation and room swaps.
- Built S3 uploads for 3 document types, with PDF validation, HEIC-to-JPEG conversion for iPhone photos, and image cropping.
- Secured sign-in with AWS Cognito, MFA and a multi-step OTP verification flow.
- Rendered long resident lists with virtualised infinite scroll.
- Held test coverage above 85%.
