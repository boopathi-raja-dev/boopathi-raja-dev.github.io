## The problem

A book distributor was replacing a legacy ERP with a new monorepo: web, mobile and desktop apps plus an API, with Indian tax support such as GST and e-way bills. Frontend and backend teams were building the same features at the same time, and their work kept colliding at the API boundary.

## What I did

- Set up the web app's foundation: file-based TanStack Router with auth-guarded routes, a TanStack Query data layer, and the shadcn/ui theme and shared layout.
- Chose shadcn/ui with a teammate after comparing the options, and documented the decision.
- Added Husky hooks across the monorepo, unasked.
- Proposed the stub-first workflow and the foundation sprint, and walked the team through both.
- Reviewed teammates' pull requests, and onboarded a new frontend teammate as their buddy.
- Contributed to shared NestJS backend modules, test-first, in a hexagonal codebase.
- Worked on-site with the client's team, with two or three calls a day.
