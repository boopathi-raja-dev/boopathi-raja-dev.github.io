## The problem

Front-desk staff juggled appointments across locations, staff rosters and service menus. The calendar had to stay fast with a busy day of bookings on screen.

## What I built

- A day/week calendar view for appointments across staff members
- Booking flows for services, staff and time slots, with validation
- Shared, reusable form and table components used across the admin app
