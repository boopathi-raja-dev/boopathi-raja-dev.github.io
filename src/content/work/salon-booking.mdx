## The problem

One booking can include several services, each needing a different staff member, while staff take leave and walk-ins arrive. The product had to make that juggling feel simple for the front desk, and let customers book and pay without calling.

## What I did

- Built the booking flow with check-in and check-out across all three apps.
- Built the staff scheduler on FullCalendar. One booking can hold several services, staff leave shows as blocked time, and custom popovers let the front desk act without leaving the calendar.
- Integrated **Stripe Connect** for online payments and **Stripe Terminal** for in-store card readers, plus Google and Facebook sign-in.
- Built marketing campaigns on 3 email templates, role-based access for staff, and Recharts dashboards for bookings and sales.
- Built the service-management form with infinite scroll and a "copy service" shortcut for salons with long menus.
