## The problem

Buyers browse large catalogues and need to find a local retailer. The marketing team needed to build and change pages without developers, and the site needed to load fast and rank well in search.

## What I did

- Raised the Lighthouse score by **20 points** with server-side rendering, SEO metadata, lazy loading and image optimisation.
- Built **25+ reusable CMS components**, so the marketing team could assemble pages themselves.
- Built a PDF catalogue generator and a Google Maps retailer search.
- Built reactive forms and virtual scrolling for large product lists, with state in RxJS and NgRx.
